# Lead guide

Show this table and ask which one they want. Do not start anything yet. Each skill tells the
booker its own plan when it starts.

| You want | Say for example | Takes about | Your dialer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leads around a booked meeting | `Leads runt Ekerö centrum` | 1-1.5 h | **Busy** only if you want the leads entered |
| Leads between two meetings | `Leads mellan Borås och Ulricehamn, möte 10:00 och 14:00` | 1-2 h | **Busy** only if you want the leads entered |
| All companies in an area (max 50) | `Alla bolag i Mölndal Kvarnbyn` | 2-4 h | **Busy** only if you want the leads entered |
| Danish leads, any of the above | `Leads omkring Aarhus Ø` | same | same |
| Check a list or one company | `Kolla Ringlista Värnamo mot Adversus` | under a minute | Free |
| Put a finished list into Adversus | `Lägg in Ringlista Värnamo i Adversus` | 1-2 min per lead | **Busy** the whole time |

**Busy** means you cannot use Adversus. Close your own dialer window before that step, Adversus
allows only one. I tell you when it is busy and when it is free again. It never dials anyone, you
do all the calling.

Every job needs Chrome open and logged in to Adversus.
