# Requirements for a valid lead

A company is a valid Blackbird Coffee lead when it meets **every** must have and **none** of the
exclusions. Same rules in Sweden and Denmark.

## Must have

| # | Requirement | How to check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **3 to 50 employees** on site | The employee figure in Adversus when the company is already there, otherwise the registry figure. When the two differ, write both in the row. 50 is a hard limit: 51 or more is out, even at 52. A band of 1-4 alone is not enough, keep it only if a page shows at least 3 people. A higher figure is fine if it covers several sites and this site is clearly 50 or fewer, say so in the row |
| 2 | **Physical premises** a salesperson can walk into | A real visiting address from the registry, the company's own site or Google Maps, not a home or a mailbox. A registry with no visiting address is not a fail on its own |
| 3 | **Staff on site during the day** | Office, workshop, shop, school, kitchen. Someone is there to taste the coffee and room for a machine |
| 4 | **Active company** | Registry status is active. Not bankrupt, in liquidation or deregistered |
| 5 | **A phone number to dial** | Copied from the company's site or a directory page. No number, no lead |

## Exclusions

| # | Not a lead | Signs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Holding companies, shells, paper entities | `Holding`, `Invest`, `Förvaltning`, `Ejendomme` in the name with 0 to 4 employees, address shared with dozens of companies, accountant's or virtual office |
| 2 | Tenants in an office hotel or coworking space | The host already serves the coffee. Drop when the address is on the country file's known list, or when 30 or more companies are registered at the same address. Flag in the row at 15 to 29 companies on one address and keep it |
| 3 | Competitors | Coffee roasteries, coffee machine suppliers, vending operators, office drinks services |
| 4 | Sole traders | Enskild firma or enkeltmandsvirksomhed, unless the registry shows 3 or more employees |
| 5 | Remote only or webshop only | No site where staff work together |
| 6 | Churches and congregations | Svenska kyrkan församlingar and pastorat, frikyrkor, Danish sogne and other religious communities |

## Valid, even if it looks wrong

Cafés, restaurants, bakeries and hotels. Their guest coffee is not the target, the staff room is.
Associations, schools and preschools also count when they meet the must haves.

## Good signs (not required)

- A named contact with a direct number on the company's own site
- Signs of a consumer-grade setup: a kitchenette, a filter brewer, a home espresso machine
- Customers waiting on site
- Found only on Google Maps, not in the registries, so competitors are less likely to have it

## Before it goes on the list

- Company ID (org.nr or CVR) copied from a registry page. If not found, it is marked `ej hittad` /
  `ikke fundet`, never guessed.
- Checked in Adversus. A company with a call of 20 seconds or more is already worked and goes in
  the second table, not the first.
